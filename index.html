Introduction

In the United States, there’s a strong focus on self-discovery — on figuring out who we are and chasing our biggest dreams. We’re often encouraged to find that perfect job or build the ideal future. It’s all part of what we call the American Dream.

But life isn’t always that simple. Maybe we have a dream, and maybe we have some idea of who we are right now. But a year from now? Our dreams might change. We might discover something new about ourselves.

And that’s OK, because life isn’t just one big picture. Life is a puzzle, made up of many different pieces. As we grow, learn, and experience more, we collect new pieces, and the picture begins to change. At twelve, your puzzle might look like an Olympic track star. At twenty, it could appear to be a math teacher. And at fifty, maybe it starts to seem like a CEO.

So don’t worry too much about what the final picture will be. Instead, focus on getting as many pieces as you can. If you do, your dream — even if it’s not the one you started with — will start to take shape, and it just might come true.

-- George
