We LOVE Summer Vacation

Summer vacation is over, but it is something that every child loves to see and enjoy. Do you agree with that? It’s either a yes or a no answer. Even some people hate the hot weather, but probably we love some part of it like eating shaved ice, eating cold noodles, boosted mood from sunlight, outdoor activities increased, going to the summer festivals, longer days for fun, family vacations and a lot more. Honestly, summer is my least favorite season of the year, but let’s look at the bright　side! The sunny weather is the perfect season for enjoying beaches, outdoor sports, fresh and seasonal fruits. Simply eating your favorite ice cream together with your loved ones (even with your lovely pets) is such a good source of love and joy that brings us together.  Keep on reading students' summerrific diary to find out!

~Melinda Kobayashi
