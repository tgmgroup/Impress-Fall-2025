About Pig Latin

Pig Latin is a fun word game that's popular in English-speaking countries. It's a "secret" code made by changing the letters of a word. There are two simple rules:

  1. If a word starts with a vowel sound ( a, e, i, o, u ), add “yay,” “hay,” or “way.”
  2. If a word starts with a consonant sound ( not a, e, i, o, u ), move it to the end and add “ay.”

Examples:

  • apple —> apple-yay
  • eat —> eat-hay
  • in —> in-way
  • outside —> outside-hay
  • umbrella —> umbrella-yay
  • pig —> ig-pay
  • cat —> at-cay
  • hello —> ello-hay
  • school —> ool-schay

It’s a silly game, but it’s pretty fun!


Pig Latin and Babigo

Actually, a lot of countries have their own special language games. Spanish people have Jerigonza, French people have Javanais, and Swedish people have Rovarspraket. Even here in Japan, there is a word game called Babigo (バビ語). The rules for Babigo are simple. Add ba, bi, bu, be, or bo after every single Japanese syllable. If you have n, add bu. So ohayou becomes obohabayoboubu and konnichiwa becomes kobonbunibichibiwaba.
